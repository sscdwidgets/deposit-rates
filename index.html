Methodology, data sources & honest limits
What the county "paid cost" is. For each institution holding deposits in the selected county (FDIC Summary of Deposits), we pull its latest quarterly call report and compute annualized interest expense on deposits ÷ total deposits. Where the deposit-specific expense field is unavailable we fall back to total interest expense and mark coverage accordingly — a proxy that can slightly overstate deposit cost for borrowing-heavy banks. County averages are deposit-weighted by each bank's in-county deposits.

The firm-wide blending limit. A call report is filed once per institution. A multi-state bank's number is its blended cost across all markets — it cannot reveal that the bank pays more in Atlanta than in a rural Florida county. Banks likely to blend across markets carry a firm-wide blended badge, and the community-bank benchmark (<$10B) is the headline figure because those institutions are mostly single-market: their firm-wide average is their local number. Read the all-institution figure as composition context, not as "the market rate."

Guideline construction. Guideline = national FDIC average for the product (via FRED; fallback estimates when a series is unavailable) + competition adjustment from county HHI + local intensity adjustment (capped, from the gap between what community banks and the blended market pay) + your stance. CD guidelines are additionally capped at the wholesale alternative (interpolated Treasury + spread) — the tool will never guide paying retail above wholesale.

Cadence & freshness. Call-report costs are quarterly and lag the current rate environment; posted-rate moves show up here only after a full quarter. Use the tracked competitor rates in Deposit Command for what specific institutions are posting this week; use this tool for the structural level of your market.

Not advice. Output is a modeling framework with editable assumptions, intended to support — not replace — pricing committee judgment. Verify every figure before acting on it.
